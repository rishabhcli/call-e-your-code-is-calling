## Devpost submission form — exact fields

These are the literal fields on the draft submission wizard, read from the live form.

### Step 1 — Project overview
- `Project name` — required, **60 character limit**
- `Elevator pitch` — required, **200 character limit**
- `Thumbnail` — JPG / PNG / GIF, 5 MB max, 3:2 ratio recommended

### Step 2 — Project details
- `* About the project`
- `* Built with`
- `"Try it out" links`
- `Image gallery`
- `Choose files`
- `* Video demo link`

Field notes:
- `About the project` — Markdown with LaTeX support. Devpost's own placeholder prompts for **Inspiration / What it does / How we built it / Challenges / Accomplishments / What we learned / What's next**.
- `Built with` — tag list, **max 25 tags** (languages, frameworks, platforms, cloud services, databases, APIs).
- `Try it out links` — repeatable URL field for demo site, app store listing, or GitHub repo.
- `Image gallery` — JPG / PNG / GIF, 5 MB max each, **up to 15 images**, 3:2 ratio recommended.
- `Video demo link` — must be a **YouTube, Facebook Video, Vimeo or Youku** URL. Embedded at the top of the project page.

## What to build / Requirements

What to Build

Build and deploy a project that uses CALL-E's SDK, API, MCP, CLI, or SKILL to create an impactful business or project use case. Submissions can be a functional application, agent skill, workflow plugin, or another reusable contribution—just make sure to provide a clear demonstration of how it works with CALL-E.

All new CALL-E accounts come with 20 free CALL-E calls. 

Running out of CALL-E calls while building your project? Request additional calls via this form.

What to Submit

- Open a pull request to CALL-E's public repository at https://github.com/CALLE-AI/awesome-phone-call-agents.

- Follow the instructions under the “README” to submit your pull request to the correct Contribution Area (i.e. “Agent Skills,” “Workflow Plugins”, etc.)

- On the Devpost submission form, provide the URL to your pull request.

- Include a demonstration video of your project (about three minutes), uploaded to YouTube or Vimeo and made publicly visible.

- Provide the email address associated with your CALL-E account

- Optional:

- Provide a URL to your functional demo application

- Have feedback for us? Please submit our CALL-E Feedback Survey and be eligible to win one of our five Most Valuable Feedback prizes!

Two repositories, two jobs — don't mix them up:

## Judging criteria

- 
 Real World Impact

 Does the project identify a real, specific phone-work problem and make a credible case that it solves it for real users? Is it worth building further for real users after the hackathon — not a generic "AI that makes phone calls" concept.
 

 - 
 Quality of the Idea

 Is this a creative, non-obvious use of CALL-E that shows genuine understanding of the problem space? Is the contribution (skill, app, plugin, or demo) clear, well-scoped, and reusable by the community?
 

 - 
 Technical Implementation

 How thoroughly and skillfully does the project use CALL-E? Does the code reflect genuine effort and a working, non-trivial implementation — CALL-E imported and actually called at runtime, not just referenced?
 

 - 
 Product Experience & Demo

 Does the project deliver a complete, coherent experience, and does the demo video clearly communicate what it does and why it matters?
